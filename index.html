About Us

Lifting Industries to New Heights

H.A Steel Elevator is a fabrication and installation company established in 2018, specializing in cargo and passenger elevators, overhead cranes, and steel shed structures. We provide complete engineering solutions including design, fabrication, installation, and maintenance services.

With a skilled technical team and industry experience, we focus on delivering safe, reliable, and cost-effective solutions in compliance with international standards. Customer satisfaction and long-term service support are at the core of our operations.
