Our Workshop

Fabrication
Done In-House

At the core of every H.A Steel project is our Karachi-based fabrication workshop. This is where raw steel becomes finished structure — cut, welded, drilled, and prepared to exact engineering drawings before anything leaves for site. Keeping fabrication under our own roof means tighter quality control, faster turnaround, and no dependency on outside vendors.

Structural steel cutting & profiling MIG & arc welding bays Drilling, grinding & surface prep Fabrication according to client drawings Quality inspection before dispatch

On-Site Operations

Built Where
It Stands

Much of our work happens directly at the client's site. Our field teams carry out on-site fabrication and installation together — meaning components are fitted, adjusted, and assembled in place by the same skilled workers who made them. This reduces errors, avoids coordination gaps, and keeps projects moving without unnecessary back-and-forth.

On-site fabrication where needed Elevator shaft & pit preparation Steel structure erection Crane runway beam installation Final commissioning & handover
